State score ranks routes by starting-material availability and route depth. Higher is better; it is not a synthesis success probability.

How to read these routes

Total reaction steps counts all reaction steps across the route, including branches. Starting materials counts the molecules at the ends of the route, before any synthesis steps; repeated molecules are counted at each position.

State score combines 95% stock coverage (the fraction of starting materials in the selected stock) with 5% depth preference (favoring fewer steps along the longest branch). It does not predict yield or laboratory success.

Solved means every starting material is in the selected stock. Unsolved routes still contain starting materials that are unavailable in that stock.
